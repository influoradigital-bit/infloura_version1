## Required fix (the minimum change that would make this gate real)

Either narrow the claim to what is actually detected, or fix four things. (1) Guard extraction must stop being a depth-0 text slice: descend into parenthesised subexpressions at the guard site (v9), follow the `if (...)` condition and early-return blocks instead of discarding them via NOT_A_CONDITION (v1), and follow the local render-helper body (v7). (2) Resolve identifiers to their local `const` initialiser before testing SETTLED/NEVER_STARTED, so `const settled = !loading && !error && everFetched` counts (v10) — without this the gate punishes the correct refactor. (3) Widen the emptiness vocabulary beyond `=== 0` / `> 0` / `.length`: `< 1`, `!== 0`, `>= 1`, `.size === 0`, and resolve copy bound to a same-file constant (v2). (4) Check discriminator polarity, not token presence — `(loading || error) && empty && <success/>` must fail as an inverted guard, not pass because the words appear. Until then the two live findings should be recorded as hand-verified defects, not as a proven detector, and the header's "one rule, structural, uncurated" claim should be dropped.
