## Brand-Side Personas

### 1. Priya — D2C Beauty Brand Founder

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28-35
- Location: Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi NCR
- Industry: D2C beauty, skincare, wellness
- Monthly marketing budget: ₹50K - ₹2L
- Revenue stage: ₹10L - ₹5Cr annual

**Pain Points:**
- Spending ₹15K-30K per post with zero guarantee creators will actually post
- Getting ghosted after advance payment (40% of the time)
- No way to track if creators actually hit their promised engagement
- WhatsApp chaos: 50+ creator DMs, screenshot negotiations, lost payment receipts
- Fake followers and engagement fraud — paid ₹25K for 2% engagement rate
- Can't scale beyond 3-4 creator partnerships because trust doesn't scale

**Search Intent (What they Google):**
- "influencer marketing platform India"
- "how to pay influencers safely"
- "escrow for influencer payments"
- "micro influencer marketing platform"
- "influencer fraud protection India"
- "beauty influencer collaboration India"

**Key Objections:**
- "Another platform asking for advance payment?"
- "Will creators actually use this or stick to Instagram DMs?"
- "Platform fees cutting into already thin margins"
- "Do I need to KYC? I don't have company registration yet"

**Primary CTA:**
- **"Start a Campaign with Escrow Protection"** — clear, safety-focused
- Secondary: "Browse 10,000+ Verified Creators"

**Messaging That Converts:**
- "Pay only when the post goes live. Money held in escrow."
- "Auto-generated contracts. No WhatsApp screenshots."
- "72-hour Hype Campaigns with 100 micro-creators from ₹50K"
- "Track every view, like, and conversion in one dashboard"

- Payment via NEFT/IMPS to random creator accounts feels risky

- "affordable influencer marketing India"
- "influencer marketing for exports brands"
- "how to build brand awareness tier 2 city"

**Key Objections:**
- "We're not a fancy D2C brand, will influencers even work with us?"
- "Our product is niche (e.g., cold-pressed oil), will we find relevant creators?"
- "GST invoices? We're still informal"
- "English-only platform, my team won't understand"

**Primary CTA:**
- **"Find Regional Language Creators in 2 Minutes"** — specificity sells
- Secondary: "Launch Your First ₹25K Campaign (Escrow Protected)"

**Messaging That Converts:**
- "1,200+ Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati creators ready to promote your brand"

- Age: 22-30
- Location: Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune (metros + tier 1)

- Spent 6 hours creating content, brand said "not what we wanted," refused to pay
- UPI payment from random brand account, no invoice, can't declare income for ITR
- Chasing brands for payment 15-30 days after posting

- "how to avoid brand payment scams"
- "micro influencer campaigns India"

**Key Objections:**
- "Do I have to pay to join the platform?"
- "Will brands actually pay on time or is this another WhatsApp nightmare?"
- "I don't have a company, can I still get paid?"
- "GST? ITR? I just want to get paid via UPI"

**Primary CTA:**
- **"Sign Up Free. Get Paid in 24 Hours."** — speed + safety
- Secondary: "Browse Active Campaigns (₹5K - ₹50K)"

**Messaging That Converts:**
- "Escrow holds brand payment until you post. Money released in 24 hours."
- "Every deal = auto-generated contract. No more WhatsApp screenshots."
- "UPI payouts. GST invoices auto-generated if you need them."
- "Brands can't ghost you. Payment locked before you create content."
- "72-hour Hype Campaigns: Apply once, get paid by 100 brands at once"

## Cross-Persona Insights

### Common Search Themes (SEO Priority):
1. **Safety/Trust:** "escrow," "payment protection," "avoid scams," "safe collaboration"
2. **Regional/Language:** "Tamil influencers," "Hindi creators," "tier 2 city," "regional brands"
3. **Affordability:** "micro influencer," "nano influencer," "affordable," "₹5K campaign"
4. **Speed:** "quick results," "72 hours," "fast payment," "instant payouts"
5. **Scale:** "100 influencers," "Hype Campaign," "bulk outreach," "manage multiple"

### Universal Objections (Homepage Must Address):
- "Is this safe?" → Escrow + contract messaging above the fold
- "Is this for me?" → Show nano/micro/regional examples in hero section
- "Hidden fees?" → Transparent pricing in header/footer
- "Another WhatsApp mess?" → "Zero WhatsApp. All in one dashboard."

### Platform-Specific CTAs:
- **Brands:** "Start a Campaign" > "Browse Creators" > "See Pricing"
- **Creators:** "Sign Up Free" > "Browse Campaigns" > "See Earnings Calculator"
- **Agencies:** "Book Demo" > "Agency Pricing" > "White-Label Waitlist"
