# User Guide — Campaigns, Bidding & AI (Meera)

> Plain-language guide for the people who actually use Influora — brands and creators.
> No code. Lead: Nisha. (Developers: the matching endpoints are in `04-API-CONNECTION.md`.)

Influora connects **brands** (who want influencer marketing) with **creators** (influencers), and protects the money with **escrow** — funds are held safely and only released when work is approved.

---

## Part A — For Brands

### 1. Get started
1. Sign up at **/brand/register**, verify the OTP sent to your email.
2. Complete **onboarding**: company details + KYC (needed before you can pay).
3. Add money to your **wallet** (/brand/wallet) via Razorpay. This is your spending balance.

### 2. Create a campaign
1. Go to **/brand/campaigns → New Campaign**.
2. Choose the type:
   - **Standard campaign** — you set the brief, budget, deliverables (e.g. "3 Instagram reels").
   - **Hype campaign** — a fast, buzz-focused format (/brand/campaigns/new/hype).
3. Fill the brief: goal, platforms, budget range, timeline, and what you want delivered.
4. Publish. Creators can now find and apply to it.

### 3. Find creators
- Open **/brand/discover**. Search and filter by niche, audience, location, and score.
- Each creator has a **score** built from their real Instagram metrics (reach, engagement, audience quality).
- **Save** creators you like, or **invite** them directly to a campaign.

### 4. How bidding works
Bidding on Influora is a negotiation, not an auction:
1. A creator **applies** to your campaign with their proposed price and terms — this is their bid.
2. You review it in **/brand/deals** (or chat). You can **Accept**, **Reject**, or **Counter** with a different price/terms.
3. The creator can accept your counter or counter back. When both agree, the deal is locked.

### 5. Contract → Escrow → Delivery → Payment
1. **Contract:** both sides sign digitally. You get a downloadable PDF.
2. **Fund escrow:** you move the agreed amount into escrow. It's held safely — the creator can see it's funded, but you haven't paid yet.
3. **Delivery:** the creator posts and submits the work with proof/metrics. You're notified instantly.
4. **Approve & release:** if it meets the brief, you approve and release escrow. The creator is paid automatically (minus the platform fee). If it doesn't, you can request a revision or open a **dispute**.
5. **Review:** leave a rating. Reviews are two-sided.

### 6. Track results
- **/brand/analytics** — performance per creator and per campaign.
- **Coupons & tracking links** — attribute real sales to each creator (great for affiliate-style deals).
- Connect your **Shopify** or **WooCommerce** store (Settings) so conversions flow in automatically.

## Part B — For Creators

### 1. Get started
1. Sign up at **/creator/register**, verify OTP.
2. **Onboarding:** connect Instagram (Meta login), fill your profile, complete KYC, and add your **bank/payout** details.
3. Build your **portfolio** (/creator/portfolio) — it gets a public page at **influora.com/yourhandle** you can share anywhere.

### 2. Find work & place a bid
1. Browse open campaigns at **/creator/campaigns**, or respond to brand invites in **/creator/deals**.
2. **Apply** to a campaign — set your price and terms. That's your bid.
3. If the brand counters, negotiate in **/creator/deals** until you both agree.

### 3. Deliver & get paid
1. **Sign the contract.** Once the brand funds escrow, your payment is guaranteed to be sitting there.
2. **Do the work,** post it, and **submit** it with the required proof/metrics.
3. When the brand approves, **escrow is released to you** — withdraw to your bank from **/creator/wallet**.
4. **Earn extra with coupons:** share your affiliate coupon; every sale it drives adds to your **affiliate earnings**.
5. Leave a **review** of the brand.

## Part C — Using the AI Assistant (Meera)

Meera lets a **brand** run the whole process by chatting in plain language. Open **/brand/meera**.

### What you can ask
- *"Find me 5 fashion creators in Mumbai under ₹20,000 each."*
- *"Create a Diwali campaign for 10 reels with a ₹1,50,000 budget."*
- *"What's a fair budget for 3 reels from a creator with 200k followers?"*

### What Meera does
1. **Understands** your request and searches real creators.
2. **Estimates budgets** and can **create the campaign** for you.
3. **Prepares payment** — but here's the safety rule: **Meera can never actually move your money.** It sets everything up and then asks *you* to confirm. Only your explicit **confirm** launches the campaign or funds escrow.

### Credits
AI usage runs on **credits** (see your balance on the Meera screen). Each conversation consumes credits; there are daily spending safeguards so costs never run away.

### Good habits
- Be specific (budget, niche, platform, count) — you'll get better results.
- Always read Meera's summary before you **confirm** — you're the final approver on anything involving money.
- You can always drop out of chat and do the same steps manually in the normal screens.

## Quick glossary
- **Escrow** — money held safely by the platform, released only when work is approved.
- **Bid / Apply** — a creator's proposed price and terms for a campaign.
- **Counter** — a revised offer from either side during negotiation.
- **Deliverable** — the actual post/reel/content the creator submits.
- **Payout** — the released escrow paid to the creator's bank (minus platform fee).
- **Coupon / affiliate earning** — extra money a creator makes from sales their code drives.
- **Meera** — the AI assistant that can run campaigns for brands via chat.
