## 1. VOICE & READABILITY STANDARD FOR LEGAL PAGES

**Goal:** Legal pages should build trust, not intimidate. Plain language where possible, legal precision where required.

### House Style Rules

1. **Plain-English summary at top of every policy**
   - 1-2 sentences max, written at 8th-grade reading level
   - Example for Escrow Policy: "We hold campaign funds safely in escrow through a licensed payment partner (Razorpay). Money is released to creators only after brands approve the work."

2. **Active voice, first/second person**
   - Use "we/our/you/your" instead of "the Platform/the User/Influora Digital Private Limited"
   - Example: "We protect your data" NOT "Influora Digital Private Limited shall protect User data"

3. **Short sentences, scannable structure**
   - Max 20 words per sentence where possible
   - Use numbered lists for multi-step processes (e.g., refund eligibility)
   - Use headers/subheaders every 3-4 paragraphs

4. **Last updated date + version**
   - Every policy page shows: `Last updated: [date] | Version [x.x]`
   - Placed prominently at top, just below page title

5. **Legal precision wins where it must**
   - Exact statutory references (e.g., "Section 194-O of the Income Tax Act") stay verbatim
   - Defined terms in quotes on first use, then plain language thereafter
   - Example: "Tax Deducted at Source ('TDS')" → then "TDS" or "tax deduction" in later paragraphs

6. **Avoid legalese jargon where a plain alternative exists**
   - "Before the campaign starts" NOT "Prior to campaign commencement"
   - "If you disagree" NOT "In the event of a dispute"
   - "We may suspend your account" NOT "We reserve the right to suspend"

## 2. CROSS-PAGE CONSISTENCY RULES

**All policies must use these terms identically. No synonyms.**

| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Influora Digital Private Limited** | Full legal entity name | Use in: footer, contact page, top of Terms. Elsewhere say "Influora" or "we". |
| **Escrow** | Campaign funds held by a licensed payment aggregator (Razorpay) until brand approves work | NEVER say "we hold funds" — we operate escrow via Razorpay. |
| **Deliverable** | The content/work a creator produces for a campaign (post, video, story, etc.) | NOT "output", "content piece", "asset" in policy context. |
| **Payout** | Money released from escrow to creator after TDS deduction | NOT "payment", "transfer", "disbursement". |
| **Campaign** | A single brand-creator collaboration project on the platform | NOT "project", "job", "deal". |
| **Grievance Officer** | The named person handling user complaints under IT Rules 2021 | Must use exact title + full name + contact. Cannot say "support team". |
| **Platform fee** | Influora's service charge (% of campaign value, subject to GST) | NOT "commission", "service charge" (only in plain-English context). |
| **KYC** | Know Your Customer — PAN/Aadhaar/GST verification required before payout | Spell out on first use, then KYC. |
| **TDS** | Tax Deducted at Source — tax withheld before payout, per Section 194-O | Spell out on first use, then TDS. |
| **Registered office** | The official company address under Companies Act | NOT "headquarters", "office address". |
| **CIN** | Corporate Identity Number | Spell out on first use in footer/contact. |
| **DPDP Act** | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 | Spell out on first use in Privacy Policy. |

## 3. TRUST & CONVERSION ANGLE

**Our policies are a selling point, not just compliance boilerplate.**

### Key Trust Signals to Emphasize

1. **Escrow = safety for both sides**
   - Frame: "Your money is protected until you're satisfied. Creators get paid fairly. Nobody gets burned."
   - Where: Escrow Policy, homepage "How It Works", onboarding FAQ
   - Tone: Confident, protective — this is our moat vs informal WhatsApp deals

2. **Licensed payment partner (Razorpay) — not us holding your money**
   - Frame: "We don't touch campaign funds. A regulated payment partner holds escrow."
   - Where: Escrow Policy, FAQ, homepage trust badges
   - Why: De-risks us in user perception (regulatory + trust)

3. **KYC/TDS = professional, tax-compliant platform**
   - Frame: "We handle the tax complexity so you don't have to. Creators get certificates. Brands stay compliant."
   - Where: TDS Policy, KYC Policy, onboarding flow
   - Tone: Adult in the room — vs shady/informal influencer deals

4. **Named Grievance Officer = we're accountable**
   - Frame: "If something goes wrong, you have a real person to contact, not a black-hole email."
   - Where: Grievance Policy, footer, contact page
   - Tone: Transparent, accessible

5. **ASCI compliance built in**
   - Frame: "Campaigns are done right — #ad labels, disclosure, no fake engagement."
   - Where: Disclosure Policy, creator onboarding
   - Tone: Premium, ethical — attracts serious brands/creators

### Where NOT to Over-Sell
- Privacy Policy: factual, not marketing. Don't say "We love your privacy!" — just explain what we collect, why, and how users control it.
- Refund Policy: clear, fair, enforceable. Don't promise "hassle-free refunds" if there are eligibility conditions.
- Terms of Service: legally binding. Plain language yes, sales copy no.
