Influora — influencer marketing platform for India

Influora is an influencer marketing platform for India. Brands hire verified creators, agree the scope and price in a shared Deal Room, and pay only after they approve the delivered work. Contracts, TDS handling and invoicing are generated automatically.

How do influencer payments stay safe on Influora?

Influora holds the brand's payment with a licensed payment partner from the moment the contract is signed, and releases it to the creator only after the brand approves the delivered work. The brand never pays in advance for work that does not arrive, and the creator never starts filming without the money already secured.

What is a Deal Room?

A Deal Room is the single shared workspace where one brand and one creator settle a collaboration: the chat, the proposal and counter-offers, the deliverables list, the e-signed contract and the payment all live in one thread, instead of being spread across WhatsApp, email and a separate invoice.

What does Influora cost?

Influora has two brand tiers: Free, which has no subscription and charges a platform fee only when a deal completes, and Pro at ₹4,999 per month, which reduces the per-deal fee and adds team seats and unlimited creator analytics. Creators join, build a portfolio and get paid for free.

What is a Hype Campaign?

A Hype Campaign is Influora's multi-creator format: a brand posts one source reel at a single flat per-reel rate, caps the number of slots, and up to 100 creators accept with one tap and post inside a 72-hour window. Each accepted slot is funded up front and paid out automatically once the post is verified.
