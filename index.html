Influora — influencer marketing platform for India

Influora is an influencer marketing platform for India. Brands hire verified creators, agree the scope and price in a shared Deal Room, approve the work, and pay once the post is live. Contracts and invoices are generated automatically.

How do influencer payments stay safe on Influora?

Influora reserves the brand's payment in their Influora wallet from the moment the contract is signed. Approving the draft clears it to go live, but pays nobody. The payment is released to the creator once their post is live and its link has been submitted. The brand never pays in advance for work that does not arrive, and the creator never starts filming without the money already secured.

What is a Deal Room?

A Deal Room is the single shared workspace where one brand and one creator settle a collaboration: the chat, the proposal and counter-offers, the deliverables list, the e-signed contract and the payment all live in one thread, instead of being spread across WhatsApp, email and a separate invoice.

What does Influora cost?

Influora has two brand tiers: Free, which has no subscription and charges a platform fee only when a deal completes, and Pro at ₹4,999 per month, which reduces the per-deal fee and adds team seats and unlimited creator analytics. Creators join, build a portfolio and get paid for free.

What is a Hype Campaign?

A Hype Campaign is Influora's multi-creator format: a brand posts one source reel at a single flat per-reel rate, caps the number of slots, and up to 100 creators accept with one tap and post inside a 72-hour window. Each accepted slot is funded up front, and Influora pays each creator by bank transfer within 2 working days of their live post link.
