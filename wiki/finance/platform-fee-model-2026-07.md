Current platform fee (15%) generates sufficient revenue at scale but leaves competitive vulnerability. **Recommended action:** Hold at 15% until 200+ deals/month, then evaluate 12% tier for volume incentive.

## 6. Scenario Modeling: Fee Tier Strategy

### Option A: Flat 15% (Status Quo)
- **Pros:** Simple, predictable revenue, no volume discount complexity
- **Cons:** Competitively vulnerable, may lose volume to 10-12% platforms
- **Best for:** Low-volume, high-margin positioning (premium Meera value)

### Option B: Tiered Pricing (Volume Incentive)
- **Tier 1 (0-100 deals/month):** 15%
- **Tier 2 (100-300 deals/month):** 12%
- **Tier 3 (300+ deals/month):** 10%

**Impact on 200 deals/month scenario:**
- Revenue at flat 15%: ₹5,46,000
- Revenue at tiered (mix): ₹4,62,000 (100 @ 15% + 100 @ 12%)
- **Loss:** ₹84,000/month (-15.4%)
- **Gain:** Higher creator retention, competitive parity

### Option C: Dynamic Fee (Meera Premium)
- **Without Meera AI:** 10%
- **With Meera AI:** 15%

**Rationale:** Unbundle the AI value. Creators who want manual browsing pay 10%. Creators who use Meera's campaign advisor, negotiation help, and analytics pay 15%.

## 7. CFO Recommendation

### 7.1 Immediate Action (Next 30 Days)
**Hold at 15%** — we don't have the deal volume data to justify a cut yet.

**Required data before any fee change:**
1. Actual monthly deal volume (last 3 months)
2. Actual monthly burn (verified, not estimated)
3. Creator churn data (how many leave due to fee complaints)
4. Meera AI usage rate (what % of creators actually use it)

### 7.2 Medium-Term Strategy (3-6 Months)
If we hit **200+ deals/month consistently:**
- Introduce **volume-based discount** (15% → 12% for power creators doing 10+ deals/month)
- Test **Meera premium tier** (10% baseline + ₹500/month for unlimited Meera AI vs. 15% all-inclusive)

### 7.3 Long-Term Strategy (6-12 Months)
- Add **brand-side fee** (5-8%) to diversify revenue
- Reduce creator fee to **12%** (competitive parity) once brand fee covers the gap
- Target blended revenue: 12% creator + 5% brand = 17% total (vs. current 15% creator-only)

### 7.4 Financial Guardrails
- **Red line:** Never drop below 10% total platform take (payment processing + ops costs = 8%, leaving only 2% margin)
- **Yellow line:** If burn coverage drops below 100% for 2 consecutive months, freeze any fee reduction plans
- **Green line:** Once at 300+ deals/month, model international expansion (fee arbitrage across markets)

## 8. Risk Factors

### 8.1 Downside of Fee Reduction
- **Revenue cliff:** Dropping from 15% → 12% = 20% revenue loss (₹1.64L → ₹1.26L on 200 deals)
- **Anchor effect:** Once we go to 12%, hard to raise back to 15% even if Meera improves

### 8.2 Downside of Staying at 15%
- **Creator churn:** If competitors launch at 10% with comparable features, we lose volume
- **Brand perception:** "Expensive platform" stigma hurts acquisition

### 8.3 Mitigation
- **Tiered model** hedges both risks: retain 15% for low-volume (margin preservation) while offering 12% for scale (volume retention)

## Appendix A: Calculation Notes

### Revenue Formula
```
Gross Revenue = (Total Deal Value) × (Platform Fee %)
Processing Cost = (Total Deal Value) × 2%
Net Revenue = Gross Revenue - Processing Cost
```

### Deal Mix Assumption
60% micro (₹5k) + 30% mid (₹25k) + 10% macro (₹100k)  
= (0.6 × ₹5k) + (0.3 × ₹25k) + (0.1 × ₹100k)  
= ₹3,000 + ₹7,500 + ₹10,000  
= **₹21,000 average deal value**

### Break-Even Calculation
```
Monthly Burn = ₹3,00,000
Net Revenue per Deal = ₹21,000 × (Platform Fee % - 2%)

Deals Needed = ₹3,00,000 / (₹21,000 × Net Fee %)

Example at 15%:
= ₹3,00,000 / (₹21,000 × 13%)
= ₹3,00,000 / ₹2,730
= 110 deals/month
```
