## THE ONE-LINE THESIS (confirmed)
India has **~20 nationally significant festivals spread across all 12 months** plus a dense regional layer — so the calendar is a *year-round* content engine, not a single Diwali spike. **60–65% of festive shoppers are now Tier-2+ cities, 85% of festive traffic is mobile,** and **vernacular/regional content is the dominant 2026 creator trend** — which is exactly why each state's own moment matters.

## VERIFICATION NOTES — what was KILLED (do not trust)

The adversarial pass refuted 3 claims — the underlying *event* is real but the *detail* is wrong:

1. **"Festive sales all run at 80–90% off"** — *killed (0/3).* The sale windows (Big Billion Days, GIF, Myntra Big Fashion Festival, Nykaa Pink Friday) are real; the blanket 80–90% discount figure is not. Don't quote discount depth.
2. **"Jan / Aug / Sep / Oct 2026 have zero Hindu wedding dates"** — *killed (0/3).* Wedding activity *dips* in these months (Adhik Maas + monsoon), but "zero auspicious dates" is false. Treat wedding-season as Feb–Jul + Nov–Dec *emphasis*, not a hard on/off.
3. **"CBSE Class-10 Feb 17–Mar 13, Class-12 Feb 17–Apr 10"** — *killed (1/2, disputed.)* Exam season is real and centers on Feb–Apr, but the exact date-sheet varies by source. Use "boards begin ~mid-Feb" until the official sheet is out.

**Also flag as low-confidence (source disagreement):** Onam main day (Aug 26 vs Sep 1), Durga Puja range (Oct 17–21 vs 19–23), Chhath (Nov 14–15 vs Nov 1–4), Navratri window. Diwali **Nov 8** and Budget **Feb 1** are high-confidence.
