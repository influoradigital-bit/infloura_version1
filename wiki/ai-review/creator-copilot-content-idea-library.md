# Creator AI Co-pilot — Content Idea Library (research-backed)

**Purpose:** the expanded set of TREND → CATEGORY → FORMAT → content-idea examples that the
co-pilot should be able to produce, so suggestions are rich and niche-specific — not "make a reel
about X." Merges **Tejas** (category/trend research) + **Zara** (format research), both from live
online research (Indian creator economy, 2026). Feeds (a) the co-pilot's example set / prompt
few-shots and (b) the theme-taxonomy expansion (§D — the actionable engineering output).

> **Scope correction:** a few researched examples drifted into B2B *export-business* content (our
> agency's own clients). This library is for **creators/influencers**, so those are recast as
> creator-native ideas. Keep that lens when adding more.

## A. Cross-category mega-trends — one trend, many niches
These are the highest-value triggers: a single real-world moment fires suggestions across *many*
creator categories at once. This is the core "one monsoon headline → a skincare idea AND a
comfort-food idea" insight, at scale.

| Mega-trend | Fans out to (category → idea → format) |
|---|---|
| **Festival** (Diwali, Holi, Navratri, Eid, Onam, Pongal, Raksha Bandhan) | Beauty → "festive glow under ₹500" *(GRWM)* · Fashion → "3 festive looks under ₹2000" *(beat-drop transition)* · Food → "Ganesh Chaturthi modak" *(tutorial)* · Home → "Diwali décor DIY under ₹1000" *(before/after)* · Devotional → "the story behind Raksha Bandhan" *(explainer)* |
| **Monsoon** (Jun–Sep) | Food → "monsoon street food at home" *(tutorial/ASMR)* · Beauty → "humidity-proof Mumbai-rain makeup" *(GRWM)* · Fashion → "monsoon outfits that actually dry" *(lookbook)* · Travel → "Lonavala 1-day monsoon trip under ₹3000" *(day-in-life)* · Fitness → "monsoon immunity shots that work" *(3 things)* |
| **Cricket** (IPL, World Cup, India–Pak) | Comedy → "types of fans during an India match" *(POV)* · Food → "15-min match-day snack platter" *(tutorial)* · Fashion → "styling a team jersey for a date" *(transition)* · Finance → "IPL auction explained for someone who failed math" *(explained-in-60s)* |
| **Budget day / tax season** (Feb) | Finance → "new tax slab for a ₹12L salary" *(explained-in-60s)* · Tech → "budget changes for salaried tech employees" *(3 things)* · Business → "ITR for freelancers, step-by-step" *(tutorial)* |
| **Big movie / OTT release** | Film → "no-spoiler first-watch reaction" *(reaction)* · Comedy → "POV: dragged to the theatre by a superfan" *(POV)* · Fashion → "recreating the lead's look on a budget" *(transition)* |

**Rule the co-pilot encodes:** a mega-trend matches many niches; a niche-specific trend (a phone
launch, an app update, an exam result) matches one. The theme-overlap score already does this — it
just needs the theme vocabulary (§D) to recognize these triggers.

## C. Format library (13 formats, 3-beat structures)
GRWM · POV · Before/After (split/wipe) · Beat-drop transition · Truck-driver lip-sync ·
Tell-me-without-telling-me · 3 Things (listicle) · Explained-in-60s · Day-in-the-life ·
Tutorial/How-to · Unboxing/Haul · Reaction/First-watch · Satisfying/ASMR.
Each = **hook (first 3s) → payoff/body (3 beats) → CTA** synced to trending audio. Full structures
in the Zara research thread (SHARED_CONTEXT / task output).

**Target quality bar** (trend + category + format + hook + 3 beats):
> *"Monsoon skin is trending — GRWM reel. Beat 1: bare skin, quiet intro ('Mumbai humidity is
> ruining my makeup'). Beat 2: cut on the beat-drop into your barrier-repair + anti-frizz routine.
> Beat 3: dewy final look, text 'monsoon-proof?', CTA 'save this for the next downpour.'"*

## D. Taxonomy additions needed (ACTIONABLE — engineering)
The co-pilot's matching runs on the closed `trendspark/theme-taxonomy.json` vocabulary. Today it's
too thin to recognize most triggers above. To make this library real, ADD theme tags in these
groups (both to the taxonomy AND to the n8n trend-tagger's keyword map):
- **Festivals:** diwali, holi, navratri, eid, onam, pongal, raksha-bandhan, ganesh-chaturthi, chhath, durga-puja, vaisakhi
- **Seasons:** monsoon, summer, winter, wedding-season
- **Events:** cricket/ipl, budget-day, tax-season, exam-season, sale-season, phone-launch
- **Categories not yet covered:** gaming, parenting, finance, automotive, pets, devotional, education, travel, home-decor
- **Regional/vernacular language tags** (see below — also the deferred vernacular gate)

Without these, a "Diwali" or "monsoon" trend row won't carry themes that overlap a creator's
`theme_tags`, so the match score stays 0 and no suggestion fires — the exact starvation the audit
would flag one layer up.
