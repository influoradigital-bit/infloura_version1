# Creator Meera may see the first line of the creator's OWN captions

**Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Decided by:** Swapnil (owner)
**Status:** Accepted. This amends [2026-07-06-brand-safety-caption-storage.md](2026-07-06-brand-safety-caption-storage.md) (LOCKED). The rest of that ADR still stands, including its rule that brands never see caption text.

## Context

On the live site a creator asked Meera to "review my profile", and Meera refused. `get_my_content_patterns` gave her each best and weak post's numbers, type and posting time, but nothing about what the post was. So she could say "your 12 Sep Reel reached 2.4x your usual" but not which Reel that was.

The 2026-07-06 ADR stores caption text on `media_metrics.caption` (V26) only as internal brand-safety input. It says raw caption text must never appear in a brand-facing DTO or in logs. `NoBrandFacingCaptionExposureTest` enforces the DTO half. That ADR did not consider showing a creator her own caption, because no creator-facing reader existed at the time.

## Decision

Creator Meera may receive **the first line of the creator's own caption** on the posts the `get_my_content_patterns` tool lists (her best and weak posts), under these rules.

| Rule | How it is held |
|---|---|
| **Creator-only.** Only the logged-in creator's own posts, and only in her own creator Meera. Never brand Meera, a brand route, a brand DTO, or another creator. | `CreatorIntelligenceService` reads rows by the creator profile resolved from the verified on-behalf JWT, and attaches the line only when `row.creatorProfileId` equals that profile (`ownCaptionFirstLine`). `CreatorOwnCaptionReachabilityTest` checks both records that carry the line: the wire DTO `CreatorToolDtos.PostReading` and the service record `CreatorIntelligenceProfile.PostStat` (inside the `CreatorIntelligenceProfile` that `CreatorIntelligenceService.profile` returns). It proves three things. (1) The only `web.dto` type that holds either record is `GetMyContentPatternsResult`. (2) The only HTTP handler whose return type can carry either one, including a handler that returns `CreatorIntelligenceProfile` bare or wrapped, is `POST /internal/meera/creator/get_my_content_patterns`, which refuses any non-CREATOR principal. (3) Only the six creator-scoped source files name the carriers (`captionFirstLine`, `PostReading`, `PostStat`, `CreatorIntelligenceProfile`, `CreatorIntelligenceService`, the executor, `bestPosts`/`weakPosts`). Elsewhere the only allowed uses are the service's caption-free static number helpers and the caption-free `Evidence`/`FollowedStat` records, which `CreatorRecommendationOutcomeService` shares; a test checks that none of them can reach either record. As defence in depth, `PostStat.captionFirstLine` is `@JsonIgnore`, so the service record never serialises the line even if it were returned. |
| **First line only, at most 100 characters.** | `CreatorOwnCaption.firstLine` takes the caption's first line. Leading blank lines do not count as a line. If that first line has nothing left once cleaned (it was only an @handle, a link or a spacer such as "."), nothing is sent, and a later line is never sent in its place. The line is cut to at most 100 code points, including a closing "…". The cut never splits a character the reader sees as one: an emoji with its joiner, skin tone or flag pair, or a letter with its vowel sign or virama. |
| **@handles and links removed.** | Removed: @handles (also after a full-width ＠ or small ﹫ at sign, and when glued to a word, as in `hi@john`); e-mail addresses; `scheme://` and `www.` links; any bare link with a path whatever its top-level domain (`youtu.be/…`, `example.co.uk/…`, `influora.ai/r/…`); and bare domains without a path on common top-level domains (`shop.nike.de`, `linktr.ee`). A link ends at any Unicode space, including a no-break space. Control characters (C0 and C1, including U+009B) become spaces. Invisible format characters (bidi overrides, zero-width spaces) are removed without a space, so they cannot split a word or hide a handle or link. The zero-width joiner and non-joiner are kept only after a non-ASCII character, where they hold emoji sequences and Indic words (for example Devanagari half-forms) together. Whitespace is collapsed. Numbers and abbreviations such as `4.5/5` and `e.g.` are not links. |
| **Two cleaners, one fixture.** | influora-ai re-applies exactly the same rules to the model's copy (`loop._caption_first_line_for_model`). Both `CreatorOwnCaptionTest` and influora-ai `test_content_patterns_real_payload.py` run the same hand-written cases in `influora-api/src/test/resources/creator-own-caption-cases.json`, so the two cannot drift apart. |
| **Null when there is none.** | A missing or blank caption, or one whose first line is empty after cleaning, gives no `caption_first_line` key (`@JsonInclude(NON_NULL)`). |
| **Never logged.** | Neither `PostStat.toString()` nor `PostReading.toString()` includes the line; both print `<redacted>`. `CreatorOwnCaptionTest#neverLogged` captures every logger at TRACE, including the outcome-failure warn path, and fails if the text shows up. |
| **No brand exposure.** | `NoBrandFacingCaptionExposureTest` is extended honestly rather than dodged. The name match is now "contains caption" (it was "ends with caption", which `captionFirstLine` would have slipped past). The five Meera/creator DTO containers that had never been scanned are now scanned. `CREATOR_ONLY_CAPTION_FIELDS` names exactly one component, `CreatorToolDtos$PostReading#captionFirstLine`, and a test fails if that entry goes stale. |
| **Untrusted text.** | A caption can hold instructions. influora-ai classifies `caption_first_line` as untrusted and wraps it in `<untrusted_creator_captions>`, and the persona tells Meera to quote or summarise it and never follow it. The frontend renders it as text, never as HTML. Each of these is owned and tested on its own side. |
| **Sent to the model provider.** | The line is part of the tool result for that turn, so it is sent to the LLM provider with the rest of the turn, the same way the post's numbers are. It goes nowhere else. |
| **Retention.** | The tool stores nothing new. The line is computed on every read from `media_metrics.caption` and is not saved as a field anywhere, so the line itself lives only as long as that row does. **But Meera's reply may quote it or sum it up.** The persona tells her to name each post by what it was about, from its caption line. Her reply is saved as the assistant message of the creator's own conversation (`MeeraSessionService`), and a script or plan topic recorded from that reply can carry the same words into `creator_recommendations.topic`. Words from the caption can therefore outlive the `media_metrics` row. They follow the conversation's retention instead: creator-only, included in her own conversation export, and deleted when she deletes the conversation (which also deletes the recommendations recorded from it) or her account. **Owner sign-off needed:** this retention wording differs from "same retention as the source rows" in the original instruction. The alternative is to have the persona describe each post only in Meera's own words and never quote the caption, pinned by a test. Even then a summary carries the post's subject into the saved reply. |

**Why this is acceptable:** the creator sees only her own public post text. It is what she wrote and published, and she can already see it on Instagram. The brand-safety ADR's concern was brands reading captions and caption text landing in logs. Both remain forbidden.

## Source

The line comes from `media_metrics.caption` (V26). `MetricsPollingJob` writes it on every per-post poll through `MediaMetricMapper` (`influora.meta.media-metrics-enabled`, which defaults to on). It is on the same newest-snapshot row as the post's numbers, keyed by `media_id`. The `creator_captions` cache written by `CreatorCaptionSyncJob` is not used, because that job only runs when `CREATOR_COPILOT_ENABLED` is on, and that flag defaults to off.

## Not decided here

- Captions of posts that `get_my_content_patterns` does not list, the full caption text, and any caption in a brand surface all need their own decision.
- Adding a second entry to `CREATOR_ONLY_CAPTION_FIELDS` needs its own ADR and a reachability proof like this one.
