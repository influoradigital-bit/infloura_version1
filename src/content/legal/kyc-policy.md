## 1. Why we require KYC

**KYC ("Know Your Customer")** verification is required under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) and RBI's KYC Master Directions, which apply to payment flows like the payment protection Payouts on Influora. We also use KYC to confirm your identity for TDS reporting and to reduce fraud on the platform.

You cannot fund a Campaign or receive a Payout until your KYC is verified.

## 2. What documents we need

**Creators (individuals):**

- PAN (Permanent Account Number) — required for TDS calculation and payout processing
- Aadhaar or another government-issued photo ID — for identity verification
- Bank account or UPI details — to receive Payouts

**Brands (businesses):**

- GST registration (where applicable)
- Company incorporation documents (CIN, registration certificate)
- Authorized signatory's PAN and ID
- Business bank account details — to fund the deal

We only collect what's needed for verification and Payout processing — see our Privacy Policy for how this data is used and retained.

## 3. Verification timeline

We aim to verify KYC submissions within **24–48 hours** of a complete, correctly submitted application. Incomplete or unclear documents will extend this timeline — we'll tell you exactly what's missing if verification is delayed.

## 4. What happens without KYC

- You can browse and set up a Campaign, but you cannot fund the deal (brands) or receive a Payout (creators) until KYC is verified.
- Unverified accounts may have limited platform features.

## 5. How we protect your KYC data

- Sensitive documents (PAN, Aadhaar, GST/company documents) are encrypted at rest.
- Access is limited to systems and personnel that need it for verification or compliance purposes.
- We do not share KYC documents with other platform users — only verification status is visible where relevant.
- Retention follows the periods required under PMLA and Indian tax law; see our Privacy Policy for detail.

## 6. Re-verification and updates

We may ask you to re-verify or update your KYC documents if:

- Your documents expire
- We detect a mismatch or suspicious activity
- Indian regulation changes and requires updated information

## 7. Rejected or failed verification

If your KYC submission is rejected, we'll tell you why and let you resubmit corrected documents. Repeated failed attempts or suspected fraudulent documents may result in account suspension, per our Terms of Service.

## 8. Your rights over this data

You can ask what KYC data we hold, request corrections, or ask about deletion — subject to our legal retention duties under PMLA and tax law. See our Privacy Policy for the full process, or contact `info@influora.in`.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as regulation or our verification process changes. We'll post updates here with a new "Last updated" date.
