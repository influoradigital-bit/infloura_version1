## 1. Why we have this policy

Under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, Influora Digital Private Limited must name a **Grievance Officer** and publish a clear, timely process for resolving user complaints.

## 3. What counts as a grievance

- A complaint about how the platform, another user, or Influora staff treated you
- A complaint about data privacy or misuse of your personal data
- A complaint about content on the platform (offensive, illegal, or infringing)
- A complaint about a policy violation you've witnessed

Campaign-specific disagreements (delivery, approval, payment protection release) go through our **Dispute Resolution Policy**, not this one. If you're not sure which applies, contact us and we'll point you the right way.

## 4. How to file a grievance

1. Email the Grievance Officer at the address above, or use the in-platform "Report a concern" form.
2. Include your account details, a clear description of the issue, and any supporting evidence (screenshots, messages, dates).
3. You'll receive an acknowledgment with a reference number.

## 5. Our resolution timeline

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgment of your grievance | Within **48 hours** |
| Resolution or clear status update | Within **30 days** |

If a grievance needs more time (for example, it requires input from a third party like our Payment Aggregator), we'll tell you why and give you a revised timeline.

## 6. What happens after you file

- We investigate the complaint, which may involve reviewing Campaign records, messages, or account activity.
- We'll update you at least once during the process if resolution takes longer than a few days.
- We'll tell you the outcome and what action (if any) we took.

## 7. If you're not satisfied

If you disagree with how your grievance was handled:

- You may escalate within Influora by replying to the resolution email and asking for a review.
- For data-related grievances, you may approach the **Data Protection Board of India** under the DPDP Act after exhausting our internal process.
- You retain any rights you have under Indian consumer protection law, including approaching a Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
